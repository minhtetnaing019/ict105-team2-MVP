# Sales Scenario

## 1. Project Title
StudyBridge — Peer Tutoring Booking Platform

## 2. Target User / Customer
- Primary user: A university student who needs help in a specific subject before an exam.
- Secondary user: A student peer tutor who wants more visibility for the sessions they offer.
- Decision-maker or admin role, if any: A student services / academic support admin who approves tutors and monitors bookings.

## 3. Situation / Context
It's 12 days before finals. A second-year student is stuck on recursion and Big-O analysis for a Data Structures course. Their usual approach — posting in the class chat group and hoping someone free responds — hasn't worked this week; everyone who replied is already busy or unavailable at the times they need.

## 4. Pain Point
The student wastes time scrolling chat history and waiting for replies that may never come, with no way to see who is actually free, what subjects they teach, or whether they're any good — and risks entering the exam without help at all.

## 5. Product Introduction
A classmate mentions StudyBridge, a simple site where you search tutors by subject and see their real availability and rating before booking, instead of asking around.

## 6. Value Message
For university students who experience unreliable, slow, chat-group-based tutor searches, our peer tutoring booking platform helps them find and book a qualified tutor in minutes by centralizing tutor search, availability, and booking into one structured flow.

## 7. Sales Conversation / Pitch Moment
"Instead of posting in the group chat and waiting, you go to StudyBridge, search 'Data Structures,' and see three tutors with open slots this week and their ratings from past students. You pick Alex Johnson, who's rated 4.9 and free Thursday at 4 PM, fill in what you're stuck on, and submit. You get a confirmation immediately, and you can check your Dashboard anytime to see the booking is Approved."

## 8. Expected User Action
- Try the demo
- Search tutors by subject
- Submit a booking request
- Check the Dashboard for booking status
- Give feedback after the session
